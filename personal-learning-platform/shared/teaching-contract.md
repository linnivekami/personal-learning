# Teaching contract · 2.0.0

A concept connects a problem, core ideas, prerequisites, reasoning relationships and applications. Teaching, cards, notes and sources link to specific concept IDs. A learner can return to the whole framework at any time.

Preferences, observed obstacles, chosen method, hint level and later outcomes are separate observations. Do not assign a permanent learning-style label. After two consecutive unresolved explanations of the same relation, change representation or repair the smallest blocking prerequisite. Success breaks that sequence; an arithmetic slip does not establish a failed explanation.

Mastery is cumulative and scoped to the assessed concept and capabilities. 0: unknown. 1: exposure, recognition or assisted success. 2: independent explanation or free application. 3: independent explanation and application, plus unassisted retention covering both after at least two days. 4: additionally independent variation. 5: additionally structurally different cross-domain transfer. Recognition, recall alone, self-report, viewing, listening, XP and imported history do not satisfy independent application.

Substantive teaching, hints and new successful learning restart the retention interval. A compound review names its reviewed abilities; consecutive review parts can cover distinct abilities without being mistaken for new teaching. Keep earned evidence; reliable unassisted delayed concept, reasoning or required-procedure failure reduces only the affected node by one level (minimum 1). Schedule a short retest in two days. Assisted remediation and unrelated recall cannot restore the lost level or clear the original weakness. Arithmetic errors and insufficient evidence do not downgrade conceptual ability.

Reading a taught explanation records exposure, never Last Verified. Assessment-first practice does not reveal teaching before the response. Attempt submission commits the event, derived state, review schedule and Next Action together with a revision check and idempotency key. A repeated assessment after feedback is assisted. Rewards are a separate ledger.

Historical grades, actual dates, hint information, sources and pending-verification flags remain archived. Imported evidence is marked legacy and never manufactures new mastery, method effectiveness or rewards. Exchange requires a versioned file and conflict preview; no silent bidirectional overwrite.

The executable implementation lives in the companion platform project (shared/core.ts, shared/types.ts and policy.json there); this Skill ships generated copies in `shared/engine.mjs` and `shared/policy.json`, used by `scripts/state.mjs`. Markdown state remains the primary record for file-based tutors; structured events are optional and, when used, must agree with `references/mastery-scale.md`. Behavioral tests establish software rules, not long-term educational efficacy.
